# Mamoot Terms of Use

_Last updated: 2026-10-21 (v0.4.1)_

Welcome to Mamoot (مموت), a small open-source text messenger. By creating an
account or using the app you agree to these terms.

## The service

Mamoot provides one-to-one and group text messaging. Secret chats are
end-to-end encrypted and bound to one device per account (like Telegram
secret chats). The service is provided free of charge, "as is", without
warranties of any kind. Availability is not guaranteed.

## Your account

- You are responsible for your account credentials, your device, your
  app-lock PIN and your backup passphrase. Secret-chat keys cannot be
  recovered by anyone — including us — if you lose them.
- One account per person; do not impersonate others.

## Acceptable use

You agree not to use Mamoot to:

- send spam, bulk unsolicited messages, or scams;
- harass, threaten, or impersonate other people;
- distribute illegal content;
- probe, overload, or circumvent the security of the service
  (rate limits, row-level security, encryption).

To keep the free service usable for everyone, automated server-side rate
limits apply (for example: limited new conversations and searches per day,
message-rate caps, and group member changes restricted to the group owner).

Accounts that violate these rules may be restricted or removed. Reports are
reviewed using the report reason and, for non-secret chats, the reported
message. Secret-chat content cannot be reviewed because it is end-to-end
encrypted; repeated reports about an account may still lead to restriction.

## Your content

You own the messages you send. To operate the service, the server stores and
delivers them as described in the Privacy Policy, including its retention
schedule for delivered secret-chat ciphertext and deleted messages.
Deleting your account removes your messages permanently.

## Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, the maintainers are not liable for
lost messages, lost keys/backups, downtime, or damages arising from use of
the service. Keep an encrypted backup if your chats matter to you.

These terms may be updated; the current version always lives in the public
repository (`docs/TERMS.md`) and in the app under Settings → Advanced
security.
